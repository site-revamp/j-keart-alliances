# J.Keart Alliances Homepage Redesign

## Website Overview

J.Keart Alliances Pte Ltd provides fire protection installation, maintenance, and compliance support for commercial and industrial buildings in Singapore. The homepage helps building owners, facilities managers, and project teams find the right service and review relevant project experience before enquiring.

## Main Improvements

1. **Give company figures labels and attribution.** The draft groups factory floor area, staff count, and projects carried out in a statistics section and attributes them to J.Keart. Confirm the figures are current before publishing.
2. **Make fire-safety services easier to find.** Dedicated cards link to installation, SCDF submission support, Fire Certificate renewals, maintenance, and specialist works. Two enquiry paths help visitors distinguish ongoing support for an existing building from installations or alterations for a new project.
3. **Give project experience and credentials dedicated space.** The draft presents dated project examples, selected maintenance sites, credentials, awards, and customer testimonials. It directs visitors to J.Keart for current credential validity. Confirm the scope and validity of the credentials before publishing.

## Design Improvements

The indigo and orange brand identity frames a service-led hero. Consistent cards and clear section headings help visitors scan services, project examples, and credentials. Call and email actions are grouped in the contact section.
